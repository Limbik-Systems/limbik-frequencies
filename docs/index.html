Privacy Policy — Limbik Frequencies

Effective Date: November 18, 2026
App Identifier: com.limbikfreq.radio
Website: https://limbikfreq.com

1. Overview

Limbik Frequencies ("we," "our," or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how the Limbik Frequencies mobile application for iOS and Android (the "App") handles information when you stream audio, customize your equalizer, submit song requests, or use Apple CarPlay / Android Auto.

2. Information We Do Not Collect

  • No User Accounts or Registration: The App does not require an account, login, name, email address, or phone number.
  • No Microphone or Camera Access: The App does not record audio, access your microphone, or access your camera. Our real-time 52-band FFT spectrum analyzer processes only the decoded radio stream audio in memory on your device.
  • No Precise Location or Contacts: The App does not request or collect GPS location, contacts, photos, or health data.

3. On-Device Preferences & Storage

The App stores your personal listening preferences locally on your device, including your preferred stream quality (320 kbps or 128 kbps MP3), bookmarked Saved Tracks, and 4-Band Parametric EQ settings / User preset. This data remains strictly on your device and is never uploaded to our servers.

4. Streaming & Station APIs (limbikfreq.com)

When you listen to the live radio stream, view track history, or submit a song request, the App connects directly to limbikfreq.com. Like any standard web server, limbikfreq.com may temporarily process standard network connection metadata (such as IP address) in server logs for stream delivery, rate-limiting song requests, and service reliability.

5. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App displays a banner advertisement powered by Google AdMob. The App is configured to request non-personalized ads only and does not request Apple App Tracking Transparency (ATT / IDFA) cross-app tracking permission. To serve and measure non-personalized ads, detect fraud, and maintain SDK stability, Google AdMob may automatically collect limited technical information such as device type, OS version, IP address (for coarse regional delivery), crash/performance diagnostics, and basic ad impression/tap interactions. See Google's Privacy Policy for details.

6. Apple CarPlay & Android Auto

When connected to Apple CarPlay or Android Auto, the App only shares playback controls and current track metadata with your vehicle's media display. It does not access or collect vehicle telemetry, driving history, or location data.

7. Children's Privacy

Limbik Frequencies is a general-audience music streaming app and does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

8. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us via https://limbikfreq.com.
